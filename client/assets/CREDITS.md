# Game art notes

The playable tavern maps, countertop atlases, ingredients, drinks, mixing
workspace, character animation, preset cat portraits, and scrap bin are
original project assets generated and refined for Paws & Pours.

The moonlit title tavern backdrop is an original generated pixel-art scene,
created to match the haunted tavern map's palette and shading.

The five bartender portraits are role presets: tabby, Siamese, Maine Coon,
black cat, and calico. Runtime fur color and accessories remain player choices.
